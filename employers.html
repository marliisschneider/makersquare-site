Set expectations first

Who these builders
actually are.

We're going to be straight with you, because it saves everyone time. MakerSquare isn't a placement program, and not every grad is job-hunting — many are operators and founders building AI into their own companies. But every cohort has people who are open to the right role, and all of them are people worth knowing.

What you get

  • Experienced operators and domain experts, not career-switchers starting from zero
  • People who can go from idea to a working AI tool without waiting on an eng team
  • Fluency in Claude, agents, and the modern build stack — demonstrated, not claimed
  • A portfolio piece: a real product they built and presented live at Demo Day

What this isn't

  • Not a coding bootcamp pumping out junior software engineers
  • Not a guaranteed-placement pipeline — we make warm intros where there's a fit
  • Not everyone is looking; many are building for their own company
  • Not a resume dump — you meet them by seeing their work, live
Why it matters

The rare hire:
deep in a domain, fluent in AI.

Most companies are stuck choosing between people who understand the business but freeze at AI, and technologists who can build but don't know the domain. MakerSquare grads are the overlap.

They ship, not theorize

Two weeks, full-time, building real tools with real data. They don't need a six-month ramp to figure out what AI can do — they've already built with it.

They bring a domain

These are people who already know your world — operations, finance, healthcare, legal, marketing, real estate. AI is the new leverage on top of experience they already have.

They de-risk the hire

You don't have to guess. You watch the product run at Demo Day and talk to the person who built it. That's a better signal than any interview loop.
