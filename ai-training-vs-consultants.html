For companies weighing the options

Hire an AI consultant,
or train your own team?

Both are legitimate answers. They just leave your company in very different places when the engagement ends. Here’s the honest version of the comparison — including when we’d tell you to hire the consultant.

Consultant engagement ends ↓
The tool they built The know-how The momentum

What they built stays. The ability to change it, fix it, or build the next one leaves with them.

Trained team ↓
First build Second build Every build after

The capability lives in your people. Every problem they solve after we leave costs you nothing extra.

When a consultant is the right call

  • •You need one complex, specialized system — and nobody internal will ever need to touch it
  • •The problem is deeply technical (custom ML, heavy integrations) rather than workflow automation
  • •Your team genuinely has no bandwidth to learn — and you accept paying again for every change

When training your team wins

  • ✓The opportunities are spread across your business — sales, ops, marketing, admin — not one system
  • ✓Your people know where the waste is better than any outsider ever will
  • ✓You want the capability to compound — every new hire, tool, and problem gets the same treatment
  • ✓You’d rather own the system than rent access to the people who understand it
