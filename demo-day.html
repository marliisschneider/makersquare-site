What Demo Day is

Not a graduation ceremony.
A product launch.

Most programs end with a certificate ceremony. MakerSquare ends with every builder on stage, in front of a live audience, presenting a product that actually runs. No slideshow. No concept pitch. A working AI product, live, with real data.

Live product demos

Every builder gets 5 minutes on stage. The product runs in real time — no pre-recorded footage, no screenshots. Real data, real Claude calls, real output. The audience sees it work or asks why it doesn't.

Real audience, real feedback

Seed-stage investors, Austin tech operators, and builders who've shipped their own products. The feedback isn't polite — it's useful. Builders leave knowing exactly what's strong, what to fix, and who in the room wants to talk more.

Connections that last

The Austin tech community that shows up to Demo Day is the same network builders tap for their next venture, job, or collaboration. The products you build are your introduction — people remember what they saw running live.
