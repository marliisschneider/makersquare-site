The AI course for product managers who want to ship the prototype themselves.

How to choose

How to choose an AI course as a product manager.

  1. Ask where the final project lives.If the answer is a slide deck or a notebook, you are buying a strategy course. That can be fine. Just know which one you are paying for.
  2. Check whether you work on your own data.Toy datasets teach the tool. Your own tickets, docs and metrics teach you where it breaks, which is the part you need at work.
  3. Look for evals and data handling on the syllabus.If you will put AI in front of customers, you need both. A course that skips them leaves you dependent on engineering for every quality call.
  4. Count the hours with an instructor.Recorded video is cheap to produce. Live help when your deploy fails is what costs money, and it is usually what gets people unstuck.
  5. Be honest about your time.A self-paced online course costs less and fits around a job. It works well if you are self-driven and need one specific skill. It tends to stall the first time something breaks and nobody is there to ask.

One case where we are the wrong pick: if your job is managing a team that trains its own machine-learning models, look for an AI product management course built around data science workflows. Ours is about building products on top of existing models like Claude.

Quick answers

AI courses for product managers, answered.

Do product managers need to code to take an AI course?

No. You will work in a terminal with Claude Code from the first week, but you describe what you want in plain English and review what it writes. Knowing your product and users matters more than syntax.

How long does an AI course for product managers take?

Ours is two weeks, full time: ten days, 9am to 3pm. If two weeks away is not realistic, our 3-day workshops cover a shorter version of the build path.

How much does it cost?

Tuition is $3,999, paid in full. Veterans, first responders and educators pay $3,499 with the scholarship. Training for a whole product team is quoted separately on our corporate page.

Will this change how I work with engineers?

It usually does. After you have deployed to Vercel, wired up a Supabase database and tested an agent yourself, your specs get tighter and you can tell a one-day change from a one-month one.
