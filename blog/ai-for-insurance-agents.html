AI for Insurance Agents: Quote Faster, Follow Up Better

Marliis Schneider September 29, 2026 5 min read
Key takeaways
  • AI adoption among insurance agents reached 64% in 2026, with independent agencies catching up fast to captives.
  • Quoting is the gateway win: AI pre-fills risk data, suggests carriers, and returns comparable quotes - cutting quote-to-bind time by 60-99% in commercial lines.
  • Downstream, AI-powered claims automation resolves claims 75% faster at 30-40% lower cost, and underwriting timelines are collapsing from days to minutes.
  • AI handles the intake, quoting, and paperwork; the advice, the relationship, and the judgment call on a tricky risk stay with the agent.

Insurance runs on paperwork and turnaround time - exactly the two things AI compresses. The direct answer: AI helps insurance agents most on the front-end grind - intake, quoting, and follow-up - where it turns hours of data entry and carrier-shopping into minutes, freeing agents to do the advising and relationship work that actually wins and keeps clients.

Adoption crossed the majority, and independents are catching up

The industry has moved decisively: AI adoption among insurance agents reached 64% in 2026, and 90% of insurers are somewhere on the generative-AI journey. Notably, independent agencies grew adoption 19 points from 2024 to 2026 - faster than captives - closing the gap that used to favor big carriers. For an independent agent, the tools that were once enterprise-only are now accessible, which is why adoption is accelerating.

Quoting: the gateway win

Quoting is, as the industry puts it, the gateway drug of insurance AI - because it has bounded workflows and measurable ROI in days. AI pre-fills risk data, suggests appropriate carriers, and returns a comparable quote sheet, collapsing a process that used to mean manual data entry across multiple carrier portals. The result: commercial P&C insurers are seeing quote-to-bind time reductions of 60-99%. When you can quote a prospect in minutes instead of days, you win business that used to go to whoever responded first.

Underwriting and claims: the bigger downstream wins

The compression continues through the pipeline. Underwriting timelines are collapsing from around 3 days to as little as 3 minutes, with straight-through processing rates jumping from 10-15% to 70-90%. On claims, insurers using AI-powered automation are resolving claims 75% faster with 30-40% cost reductions, and fraud detection has improved over 30%. For an agent, faster underwriting and claims means happier clients and less time spent chasing status - the servicing work that eats a book of business.

What stays with the agent

The line is clear and it's where an agent's value concentrates. AI handles intake, quoting, data entry, and status - the repetitive front and back office. It does not replace the advice on what coverage a client actually needs, the judgment on an unusual risk, or the relationship that makes someone renew and refer. As AI absorbs the paperwork, the agent's time flows to exactly the high-value work: advising, relationship-building, and handling the situations that don't fit a template. The tool makes you faster; you stay the expert.

What this means for agents building AI skills

The agents who benefit most understand their clients and carriers well enough to direct AI precisely and keep it to the operational layer - which is judgment, not technical skill. That's the MakerSquare premise. Our use cases show what building tools around a real professional workflow looks like.

Nobody renews with an agent because the quote came back fast. They renew because of what you did with the time that speed gave you.

Frequently asked questions
How are insurance agents using AI in 2026?
Mainly on the front-end grind: intake, quoting (pre-filling risk data, suggesting carriers, returning comparable quotes), and follow-up, plus faster underwriting and claims servicing. AI adoption among agents reached 64% in 2026, with quoting typically the first and highest-ROI use case.
How much faster is AI quoting for insurance?
Substantially - commercial P&C insurers report quote-to-bind time reductions of 60-99%, and underwriting timelines are collapsing from about 3 days to as little as 3 minutes with straight-through processing rising from 10-15% to 70-90%. Quoting a prospect in minutes instead of days directly wins business.
Will AI replace insurance agents?
No. AI handles intake, quoting, data entry, and claims status - the repetitive office work - but the advice on what coverage a client needs, the judgment on unusual risks, and the relationship that drives renewals and referrals stay with the agent. AI concentrates an agent's time on that high-value work.
Does AI help with insurance claims?
Yes - insurers using AI-powered claims automation resolve claims 75% faster with 30-40% cost reductions, and fraud detection has improved over 30%. For an agent, faster claims processing means happier clients and less time spent chasing status on their behalf.
Do insurance agents need technical skills to use AI?
No. AI insurance tools are built around agent workflows and used in plain language. The valuable judgment - directing AI on quoting and intake while keeping advice and relationships human - comes from knowing your clients and carriers, not from technical ability.
