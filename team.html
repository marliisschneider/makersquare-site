The team.

Founder & CEO
Marliis Schneider

Marliis has spent years in the operational trenches. Running teams, building systems, and watching firsthand as AI tools went from novelty to necessity in real businesses.

She built MakerSquare because she kept meeting smart, experienced operators who desperately wanted to use AI but had nowhere to actually learn it in depth. Her background spans operations, business development, and startup ecosystems.

Deeply embedded in Austin's tech and founder community. At MakerSquare, she sets the program vision, owns the curriculum direction, and runs the relationships that make Demo Day matter for graduates.

Austin, TX Operations Business Development Startup Ecosystems
Co-Founder
Ravi Parikh

Ravi and Marliis co-founded the original MakerSquare — one of the most respected coding bootcamps in the country. The program trained thousands of developers before being acquired by Hack Reactor. That track record, and that Austin network, is what the MakerSquare AI program is built on.

After MakerSquare, Ravi founded RoverPass, a booking and management platform for RV parks and campgrounds that he runs as CEO today, and later Parikh Financial. He's spent the last decade building capital-efficient companies from Austin and embedding AI into every team he leads.

He rejoined forces with Marliis to bring MakerSquare back — this time not to train developers, but to turn experienced operators and domain experts into AI builders. The timing is finally right.

Austin, TX Serial Entrepreneur Parikh Financial RoverPass AI-First Operations
