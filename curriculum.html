Before Day 1

Four tasks. About an hour.

The rule is gather, don't build — so Day 1 starts at full speed.

01

Set up your accounts

Claude, GitHub, Vercel, Supabase, Warp, and the Anthropic Console (with $10 in API credits). We send exact links.

02

Have your first expert conversation

Talk to Claude about your field like an expert would. Save the conversation — you'll use it on Day 1.

03

Know your problem

Four short written answers: what the problem is, who it affects, what inputs it needs, and what "solved" looks like. This is also our fit check.

04

Bring your Materials Kit

A real dataset, a few documents, and a list of about 10 real inputs (redacted). Every hands-on day runs on your own material, not toy data.
