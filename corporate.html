The program

Learn. Build. Ship. No fluff.

Full days, Mon-Fri. On-site at your office or in Austin. Length is scoped to your program during discovery.

Phase 1 · Learn

How AI works. Applied to your work.

  • How AI models work (the intuition, not the math)
  • Prompt engineering for real business tasks
  • AI applied to your team's actual daily work
  • Identify automation opportunities across the org
  • Scope the project your team will build in Phase 2
End of Phase 1: Every participant has integrated AI into their actual job. Project scope approved by leadership.
Phase 2 · Build

Build it. Ship it. Present it.

  • Full build sprint on the scoped project
  • No-code and low-code AI tools
  • Connect to your real data and systems
  • Test, refine, document for maintenance
  • Present the deployed tool to leadership
End of Phase 2: A deployed, working AI tool. Presented live to leadership. Ready to use from day one.
Custom packages

We build the program around your team.

Not every company needs the same thing. We work with you to scope the right team, the right content, and the right length — before anything is scheduled.

Whole-company rollout

Train every team in sequential waves. Each cohort builds something for their own function — sales, marketing, ops, finance — and leaves with a tool built for how they actually work.

Team-specific intensive

Your sales team has different problems than your marketing team. We scope the content specifically for one function — the tools, workflows, and use cases that matter most to that group.

Custom length & curriculum

Two weeks is the default. But we can scope a shorter sprint for a specific problem or a longer engagement if there's more to build. We help you figure out exactly what fits before anything is committed.

How we scope it together

We start with a 45-minute discovery call — you and 1–2 people who know the operations. From that, we identify the highest-value problems, which teams should go first, and what the format should look like. No long sales process. Most companies go from first conversation to Day 1 in under 3 weeks.
