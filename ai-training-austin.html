The honest comparison

Why in-person, when every
online course is cheaper?

An online course is genuinely the right call if you need one specific skill and you're self-driven — there are good ones, and they cost a tenth of this. Where they fail is where most people actually get stuck: the moment something breaks and there's no one in the room. Our whole model is being in the room — an instructor at your shoulder for two weeks, a cohort building next to you, and your own real work as the material. That's also why we're in Austin and not on Zoom: the room is the product.

This is what training here looks like.

Quick answers

AI training in Austin, answered.

Is it really in person?

Yes — fully. The 2-week intensive runs Mon–Fri, 9am–3pm in Austin, a small cohort. Team programs run on-site at your office or at MakerSquare.

Do I need a technical background?

No. It’s built for operators, founders, consultants, and domain experts. Cohort 1 came in without coding backgrounds and left with deployed products.

What do I actually leave with?

A live website, a working AI agent, and an automated workflow — deployed, built on your own work, presented at Demo Day on the final Friday.

Do you train company teams?

Yes — private 3-day workshops and custom programs for teams of 3 to 15, built around your tools and workflows. See corporate training → or read how an Austin company runs on what we teach →
