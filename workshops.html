No. These workshops are designed for professionals who are experts in their field, not engineers. If you can use a spreadsheet, you can build in these workshops.

You're building with your actual data, your actual tools, and your actual problem — not a made-up exercise. And when you get stuck, a real instructor unblocks you in real time. Online courses don't do that.
What if I can't commit to 3 full days?
Each day builds on the last — missing one means missing foundational work. If 3 consecutive days is a stretch, the private team format lets you break it into a custom schedule.

Yes. Email marliis@makersquare.ai and we'll send a proposal and invoice within 24 hours. PO-based payment is supported.
