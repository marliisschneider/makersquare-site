The shift

The wall just came down.

For fifty years, building software meant being a developer. That wall is gone — like the Berlin Wall came down, the barrier between having an idea and shipping it just fell. If you can describe what you want, you can build it, and automate almost anything you do in a day. No CS degree. No engineering team. Your expertise and the right tools are enough now.

Who it's for

We don't teach engineers.
We teach people who know their field.

Operators & business owners

Half your week is repeatable. Build the AI that does it.

Proposals, reports, follow-ups, invoicing — you already know which parts of your business should run themselves. Knowing what to automate is the hard part. Building it is now learnable.

Professionals & career builders

Domain expertise plus AI is the rarest profile in your field.

Marketing, finance, healthcare, law — every industry is short on people who understand the work and can also build with AI. That combination is the career moat.

Founders & first-time builders

Stop waiting for a technical co-founder.

If you can describe the product clearly, you can ship the first version yourself. The idea and the market knowledge were always the scarce part.

Not the right fit if…
— You're after a certificate, not a working product — You want self-paced videos, not a room full of builders — Two full weeks in Austin isn't possible right now — You'd rather study the theory than ship
