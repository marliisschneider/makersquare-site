Real problems. Real builds.

Two of our August graduates — and the working tools they walked out with, running on their own jobs.

Chris Cox
Director of System Performance · Hesed House (homeless shelter)
What he built
An ops dashboard plus two agents for the shelter — one that scouts grant and funding opportunities, one that finds voucher-friendly housing for guests — each dropping a weekly digest in his inbox, so staff stop hunting ten sites by hand.
“The biggest win isn’t what I built — it’s what I can do going forward. There’s nothing they could hand me that I couldn’t build a solution for.”
Jennifer Francis
Construction & Facilities Project Management
What she built
An agent that handles every incoming RFI and submittal email — reads it, downloads the file, files and renames it to spec, and logs it to the right project hub. Four manual steps on every email, gone.
“I could take a real-world problem, build a solution for it, and do it in two weeks — something that felt unfathomable before.”
