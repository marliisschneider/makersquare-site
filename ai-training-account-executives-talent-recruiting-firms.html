Your job, with AI

What you do today.
What you can do after training.

We read account executive job postings from staffing agencies, recruiting firms, and talent marketplaces and pulled out the six parts of the job they all share. Each one sits next to what you can do once you’ve built your own AI tools with us. Sales and recruiting teams already run every one of these.

Your jobAfter MakerSquare training
Your job
Find and open new accounts

You research target companies, make high-volume calls, emails, and LinkedIn touches, work referrals, and reactivate former clients to keep the pipeline full.

After MakerSquare training

Know which accounts are hiring before you pick up the phone

Every morning your agent checks your target accounts’ career pages and news for the roles your firm fills, ranks them by fit, and drafts an opener that names the actual openings. It can lead with an anonymized profile of your strongest candidate. You approve what gets sent.

Your job
Run intake and discovery calls

You meet hiring managers, often 10 or more a week, to learn the role, budget, timeline, and must-haves, then turn that into a job order your recruiters can work.

After MakerSquare training

Turn every intake call into a complete job order

Your call transcript fills in every field of your intake form: skills, pay or bill rate, schedule, interview steps, deal-breakers. It lists the questions you forgot to ask. Any change to a rate or salary waits for your approval before it touches the CRM.

Your job
Deliver candidates with your recruiters

You pass requirements to the recruiting team, present candidates, coordinate interviews, collect feedback, and manage offers.

After MakerSquare training

Get a ranked shortlist with the case already written

Your tool reads the job order, searches your candidate database, ranks the best matches, and drafts the submittal note on why each one fits, plus the emails to schedule interviews and chase feedback.

Your job
Negotiate and close

You negotiate rates, contracts, and terms, and get the client to a signed agreement.

After MakerSquare training

Quote with the numbers in front of you

Before the call, you get a one-page brief: past orders, rates you have filled at, your margin floor, and what the client said on earlier calls. After it, a one-page recap the hiring manager can forward to whoever signs.

Your job
Grow and keep accounts

You check in after placements, run business reviews, solve problems early, and find the next order inside each client.

After MakerSquare training

Catch churn early and spot the next order

A weekly check flags assignments ending soon, clients whose orders dropped, and accounts posting roles you didn’t get. New placements get check-ins at day one, week one, and month one, with your follow-up drafted.

Your job
Keep the CRM and reports current

You log every call, meeting, and next step, and report pipeline and activity to your manager each week.

After MakerSquare training

Stop typing your week into the CRM

An hourly agent drafts replies to client emails in your voice. Another reads your calls and email, fills in stages and next steps, and builds your Friday pipeline report for you to add commentary.

How you learn it

You build on your real accounts

You describe what you want in plain English, and Claude Code writes the code. We teach you how to check it, fix it when it breaks, and deploy it. Composio connects your tools to Gmail, Slack, and CRMs like HubSpot, Salesforce, and Zoho. GitHub Actions runs the daily jobs on a schedule. An instructor works next to you the whole time, and each tool you build runs on your own email and pipeline by the time you leave.

Questions

AI training for account executives, answered

How can an account executive learn AI?

Build something for your own pipeline. A research agent for your own prospect list teaches you how the tools work and leaves you with something you use every week. All four of our formats work this way: weekend workshop, 2-week immersive, private team training, and 1:1.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Most of our students have never written code. You describe what you want in plain English, and we teach you to check, fix, and deploy what Claude Code writes.

Will it work with our CRM?

Usually. We connect tools through Composio, which works with HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho, Gmail, Slack, and hundreds of other apps. Tell us your stack on the intro call and we'll confirm.

Can my whole sales team train together?

Yes. Private team training is for groups of 3 to 15 and runs at your office, built around your team's tools. We quote it after the intro call. More on team training →

Is it online?

The weekend workshop and the 2-week immersive are in person in Austin. 1:1 training can be in person or online. Private team training happens at your office.

What does it cost?

The 2-week immersive is $3,999 and the weekend workshop is $1,399. Team and 1:1 training are quoted after the intro call.

Tell us about your pipeline.

On the intro call we look at your role and your team, then suggest the workshop, the immersive, team training, or 1:1. If none of them fit, we'll tell you.
