What an account executive can build after training

We took a real job posting for a full-cycle AE at a talent marketplace and went through it one responsibility at a time. Each row is part of the job, next to a tool you could build for it.

The job description saysAfter training, you build
The job description says
Run a high-velocity sales motion

Work inbound and outbound leads fast, research every company, and hit tight first-response times on new leads.

After training, you build

Lead-response agent

When a new lead lands in your CRM, it reads the company's website, open roles, and recent funding news, then drafts your first reply using what it found. You check it and send it within minutes.

The job description says
Own discovery, pitch, and close

Run discovery calls, work with the talent team to present recommended freelancers, coordinate interviews, and get the client to a signed contract.

After training, you build

Call-to-brief pipeline

Your call transcript becomes a talent brief for the matching team. When candidates come back, it drafts a short note on why each one fits and the emails to schedule interviews.

The job description says
Outbound when pipeline is light

Re-engage past leads and dormant accounts in the CRM, and send targeted email and LinkedIn outreach to new accounts.

After training, you build

Dormant-deal revival

A weekly job pulls stale and closed-lost deals, checks which of those companies are hiring again, and drafts a personal note that mentions what changed since you last spoke.

The job description says
Light post-sale ownership

Hand off closed deals with clear scope and success metrics, check in with active clients, and flag churn risk early.

After training, you build

Handoff doc and account watch

It writes the handoff doc from your deal notes. Each week it flags clients whose hours dropped, whose replies slowed, or whose trial is ending, and drafts the check-in email.

The job description says
Operate from the data

Keep deal stages, next steps, and close dates accurate in the CRM, and share patterns from calls with sales, talent, and ops.

After training, you build

CRM updater and Friday summary

It reads your emails and call notes and fills in stage, next step, and close date. Every Friday it sends a summary of the objections and requests that came up across your calls.

How you learn it

You build on your real accounts

You describe what you want in plain English, and Claude Code writes the code. We teach you how to check it, fix it when it breaks, and deploy it. Composio connects your tools to Gmail, Slack, and CRMs like HubSpot, Salesforce, and Zoho. GitHub Actions runs the daily jobs on a schedule. An instructor works next to you the whole time, and each tool you build runs on your own email and pipeline by the time you leave.

Questions

AI training for account executives, answered

How can an account executive learn AI?

Build something for your own pipeline. A research agent for your own prospect list teaches you how the tools work and leaves you with something you use every week. All four of our formats work this way: weekend workshop, 2-week immersive, private team training, and 1:1.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Most of our students have never written code. You describe what you want in plain English, and we teach you to check, fix, and deploy what Claude Code writes.

Will it work with our CRM?

Usually. We connect tools through Composio, which works with HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho, Gmail, Slack, and hundreds of other apps. Tell us your stack on the intro call and we'll confirm.

Can my whole sales team train together?

Yes. Private team training is for groups of 3 to 15 and runs at your office, built around your team's tools. We quote it after the intro call. More on team training →

Is it online?

The weekend workshop and the 2-week immersive are in person in Austin. 1:1 training can be in person or online. Private team training happens at your office.

What does it cost?

The 2-week immersive is $3,999 and the weekend workshop is $1,399. Team and 1:1 training are quoted after the intro call.

Tell us about your pipeline.

On the intro call we look at your role and your team, then suggest the workshop, the immersive, team training, or 1:1. If none of them fit, we'll tell you.
