Your desk, with AI

What you do today.
What you can do after training.

We read recruiter postings from staffing agencies, search firms, and industrial and IT staffing desks. Full desk or delivery desk, the same six parts of the job showed up. Each one sits next to what you can do once you’ve built your own AI tools with us.

Your jobAfter MakerSquare training
Your job
Take the order and check it’s fillable

You confirm the scope, must-haves, pay or bill rate, start date, and interview steps with the account manager or client before you spend a day sourcing.

After MakerSquare training

Know if an order is fillable before you start

Your tool reads the job order and checks it against your own past placements: what similar roles paid, how long they took, which must-haves knocked out most candidates. It drafts the questions to send back before you burn a day on a search that can’t close.

Your job
Source candidates

You search your database, LinkedIn, job boards, and referrals, and keep a bench warm for the roles your desk fills most.

After MakerSquare training

Start every order with a ranked list from your own database

It searches your ATS for past candidates and contractors whose assignments end soon, ranks them against the must-haves, and quotes the line from each resume that earned the match, so you can check the reasoning in seconds.

Your job
Reach out and screen

You call, text, and email candidates all day, then screen for skills, availability, pay expectations, and work authorization.

After MakerSquare training

Send outreach that mentions their actual work

It drafts a short message for each candidate that references their real experience, and checks your inbox and ATS so nobody gets contacted twice. After a screen, your call notes fill in skills, availability, pay, and authorization for you.

Your job
Submit candidates

You write the candidate summary, reformat the resume into the client’s template, and get it to the account manager or client before another agency does.

After MakerSquare training

Send a client-ready submittal in minutes

It combines the job order, the resume, and your screening notes into the client’s format with a short fit summary. You read it, adjust anything that sounds off, and send it.

Your job
Prep, debrief, and close

You prep candidates for each interview, debrief both sides, handle counteroffers, and get the offer accepted and the start date confirmed.

After MakerSquare training

Never let a candidate go quiet between rounds

Each interview gets a prep sheet built from the client’s past feedback. A daily check flags anyone who hasn’t heard from you in two days and anyone who mentioned another offer, with your follow-up drafted.

Your job
Keep the ATS current and redeploy

You log every call and submittal, check in on people on assignment, and line up their next role before the current one ends.

After MakerSquare training

Redeploy contractors before they walk

Every Monday you get a list of assignments ending in the next 30 days, each matched to open orders that fit. Notes from your calls and emails land in the ATS without you typing them.

Questions

AI training for recruiters, answered

How can a recruiter learn AI?

Build something for your own desk. A tool that ranks candidates from your database for a live order, or drafts submittals from your screening notes, teaches you how AI works and saves you time the week you finish. All four of our formats work this way: weekend workshop, 2-week immersive, private team training, and 1:1.

Will AI replace recruiters?

It replaces the prep: searching, reformatting, and logging. Clients still hire agencies for the conversations, the judgment about who will actually show up and stay, and the close. Recruiters who build their own tools get more of their day back for that work.

Is it safe to use candidate data?

The tools you build run under your own logins and work with your ATS and inbox, so candidate records stay where your agency already keeps them. Nothing goes to a client or candidate without your approval. Check your agency's AI policy first, and we'll help you build within it.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Most of our students have never written code. You describe what you want in plain English, and we teach you to check, fix, and deploy what Claude Code writes.

Will it work with Bullhorn or our ATS?

Usually. Bullhorn, Recruiterflow, JobAdder, and most ATS platforms offer API access on some plans, and Gmail, Outlook, and Google Sheets connect easily. Tell us your stack on the intro call and we'll check.

Can our whole recruiting team train together?

Yes. Private team training is for groups of 3 to 15 and runs at your office, built around your ATS and the roles you fill. We quote it after the intro call.

What does it cost?

The 2-week immersive is $3,999 and the weekend workshop is $1,399. Team and 1:1 training are quoted after the intro call.

Tell us about your desk.

On the intro call we look at the roles you fill and how your agency works, then suggest the workshop, the immersive, team training, or 1:1. If none of them fit, we’ll tell you.
